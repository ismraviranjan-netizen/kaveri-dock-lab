# SOP 214 — Customs holds and e-way bill re-filing

Kaveri Freight & Fulfilment Pvt. Ltd., Pune. Operations desk standard operating procedure 214, revision 7, reviewed weekly by the compliance cell. This document tells a dispatcher what to do when a consignment is stopped at an inland container depot or a port because its paperwork does not match the goods. Read sections one and two before touching any consignment. Sections three and four are the two procedures most often confused: cancellation and re-filing. They are different actions with different consequences, and picking the wrong one costs the customer a day.

## 1. What a customs hold is

A customs hold means the officer at the depot has stopped the consignment and will not release it until the paperwork is corrected. The most common reason at Pune is a mismatch between the e-way bill and the invoice: the vehicle number has changed, the value has been edited, or the bill expired while the truck waited. A hold is not a seizure. The goods stay in the yard under the depot's care, and the customer is told the consignment is delayed, not lost. The dispatcher's first job is to find out which document the officer has flagged. Open the consignment in the TMS, read the hold code, and note the depot name and the officer's remark exactly as written. Hold codes at Pune are three letters: EWB for an e-way bill problem, INV for an invoice problem, VEH for a vehicle problem. The code tells you which section of this SOP applies, so do not skip it.

## 2. First checks before any action

Confirm the consignment id with the customer or the portal before you do anything. Check the e-way bill number against the invoice number and the vehicle registration on the gate pass. Check the bill's validity window: a bill is valid for one day per hundred kilometres of distance, and the clock does not stop while the truck waits at the yard. If the bill is still valid and only the vehicle number is wrong, you need a vehicle update, not a cancellation and not a re-filing. If the bill has expired or the invoice value is wrong, continue to section four. If the goods are not moving at all because the order was withdrawn, continue to section three. Write the reason you chose in the consignment notes before you start, with the section number, so the next shift and the audit can see why.

## 3. Cancellation of an e-way bill

Cancel an e-way bill only when the goods will not move under that bill at all. Typical reasons: the customer cancelled the order, the goods were loaded on the wrong truck and never left the warehouse, or a duplicate bill was generated by mistake. Cancellation is possible only within twenty-four hours of generation and only if the bill has not yet been verified by any officer in transit. A bill verified at a check post cannot be cancelled by Kaveri; the recipient must reject it on their side instead. To cancel, open the e-way bill portal, choose cancel, enter the bill number and the cancel reason code, and confirm. Save the cancel acknowledgement to the consignment file. A cancelled bill cannot be revived. If you cancel a bill for goods that must still move, you will have to generate a fresh bill from scratch and the consignment will wait at the yard until it exists. Do not cancel during a customs hold unless the officer has told you in writing that the goods are being returned. Cancellation during a hold is the single most common dispatcher mistake recorded in this SOP's history, and it is why the cancel and re-file procedures sit next to each other here.

## 4. Re-filing an e-way bill after a hold

Re-filing is the correct action when the goods must still move but the current e-way bill is expired, has the wrong value, or carries the wrong invoice. The old bill is not cancelled; it is extended or replaced, and the consignment keeps its id. Step 1: pull the officer's remark from the TMS hold record and identify which field is wrong. Step 2: ask the billing desk for a corrected invoice if the value or the consignee is wrong; if only the validity has lapsed, no new invoice is needed. Step 3: on the e-way bill portal, choose extend validity if the bill expired within the last eight hours, otherwise choose generate new and quote the original bill number in the remarks field. Step 4: enter the corrected invoice number, the value, the vehicle number from the gate pass, and the depot as the current location. Step 5: submit, download the new bill, and attach it to the consignment in the TMS with the tag re-filed. Step 6: send the new bill number to the depot officer by the official email channel and record the time sent; the hold is lifted only when the officer confirms, which usually takes two to four hours. Do not tell the customer the hold is cleared until that confirmation arrives.

## 5. Vehicle and transporter updates

If the only error is the vehicle registration or the transporter id, use the update vehicle option on the portal. This keeps the same e-way bill and does not count as re-filing. Enter the new vehicle number, the reason for change, and the place where the change happened. Updates are allowed any number of times while the bill is valid. The gate pass at the depot must be reissued to match. Tell the driver to carry both the old pass and the new one until the truck has cleared the depot gate, because the officer may ask for either.

## 6. Communicating with the customer

Message the customer within thirty minutes of the hold being recorded. State the consignment id, the depot, the reason in one plain sentence, and the next time you will update them. Never quote the officer's remark verbatim. Never promise a release time before the officer has confirmed. For consignments above fifty thousand rupees in value, also call the customer; a message alone is not enough. Log every message in the consignment timeline so the next shift can see what was promised. If the customer replies with a question you cannot answer from this SOP, say so and give a time by which you will have the answer; a wrong promise costs more than a late one.

## 7. Escalation

Escalate to the operations lead if the hold passes eight hours without a confirmed reason, if the officer asks for a document Kaveri does not have, or if the customer threatens cancellation of the order. Escalate to the compliance cell if any correction would change the declared value by more than ten percent. Escalation is a tool call for the agent and a phone call for a human; both must be recorded in the timeline with the time and the person reached.

## 8. Message templates

Hold notice: Your consignment KF-XXXX is held at depot name for a paperwork check. We are correcting the e-way bill and will update you by time. Release notice: Your consignment KF-XXXX has been released by customs and is moving again. Expected delivery is date. Cancellation notice: As requested, consignment KF-XXXX has been cancelled and the e-way bill withdrawn. Use the templates exactly; do not add apologies the company has not approved.

## 9. Audit and record keeping

Every hold must leave four records: the hold code, the reason chosen in section two, the acknowledgement from the portal action taken, and the officer's release confirmation. The compliance cell samples ten holds a week and reads the timeline from the first message to the release confirmation. A hold with a missing record is counted as a process failure even if the consignment was delivered on time.
